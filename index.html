Why We Use Transmitters

📏

Distance

Raw sensor signals (mV, Ohm) degrade over meters. Transmitters send robust signals (mA) over kilometers.

🛡️

Noise Immunity

Current loops are highly resistant to EMI from motors and heavy machinery in factory environments.

🔋

Live Zero

Starting at 4mA allows instant detection of wire breaks (0mA), distinguishing faults from zero readings.

🧠

Standardization

Standardized output allows a PLC to read temperature, pressure, or flow using the exact same input card.
